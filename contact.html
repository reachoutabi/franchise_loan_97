Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about initiating financing with EquiFund Capital.

How quickly will a commercial credit officer review my submission?

Inquiries received during operating hours are assigned to a specialized loan officer within 30 minutes. If your request includes an equipment quote or vendor invoice, we can often generate indicative syndication term sheets within 4 hours.

Does reaching out or submitting this form impact my personal credit score?

No. Our initial consultation and pre-qualification utilize soft credit pulls only, which have zero impact on your personal FICO score or commercial Paydex rating. Hard inquiries occur only upon your explicit written authorization when final contracts are prepared.

Can I speak with an underwriter before I have chosen an equipment vendor?

Absolutely. Many clients establish pre-approved multi-draw leasing credit lines first so they can negotiate with auction houses, dealerships, and machinery manufacturers with verified funding in hand.

What documentation should I prepare for our initial discussion?

For transactions under $250,000, we operate on an "Application-Only" basis requiring only your business application and 3 months of commercial bank statements. For larger syndications ($500k to $5M+), having your past 2 years of business tax returns and an interim P&L will expedite term sheet generation.
